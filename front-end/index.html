ABOUT IKKO ACADEMY

IKKO Academy is the official digital dojo of the International Kenbukai Karate & Kobudo Federation — every kihon, kata, kumite and conditioning lesson taught in order, by real instructors, so you train with the same structure and correction as students on the mat.
